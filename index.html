About

I got into data work during my internship at UiTM Global, where I built dashboards tracking international partnerships across the university system. Seeing raw spreadsheet data turn into something a director could actually use in a meeting changed how I think about software.

Now I split my time between data analytics and building full-stack applications. On the data side, I work with Looker Studio, Power BI, and Python. On the development side, I build with Java, JavaScript, and frameworks like Spring Boot and Express.js. I also have hands-on experience with TensorFlow and Keras from AI coursework.

I speak Malay natively, English professionally (MUET Band 4.0), and I am picking up Mandarin through three completed levels at UiTM.
